Common questions.

How is this different from the weight-loss program?+

Same medication class, different intent. Weight-loss dosing escalates to drive fat loss. Microdosing holds a low, steady dose aimed at inflammation and metabolic health, with weight change as a secondary effect.

Will I still lose weight?+

Many patients do, more slowly and more sustainably. If your primary goal is significant weight loss, your provider will likely recommend the full-dose program instead.

Are side effects lower at a microdose?+

Generally yes. Most GLP-1 side effects appear during dose increases, and a microdosing protocol avoids the aggressive escalation. Your provider still monitors and adjusts.

Can I switch between protocols?+

Yes. Billing is month to month and your provider can move you between weight-loss and microdosing protocols as your goals change.
