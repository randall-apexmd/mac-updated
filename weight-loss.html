Get the answers you need.

What are GLP-1 medications?+

GLP-1 receptor agonists were originally developed for type 2 diabetes and have also been shown to support significant weight loss. They mimic a natural hormone that helps regulate appetite, blood sugar and digestion, so you feel fuller longer with fewer cravings.

How do I choose between semaglutide and tirzepatide?+

Both are effective, but they work differently. Semaglutide targets appetite regulation; tirzepatide works on two metabolic pathways and may lead to greater fat loss for some people. Your provider will help you decide based on your goals, history and response.

Will I lose muscle or bone density?+

Muscle loss is not inevitable. We emphasize adequate protein, resistance training and appropriate dosing to protect lean mass and bone health while you lose fat. Working with a M.A.C. trainer is one of the best ways to hold onto muscle through the process.

When will I see results?+

Many patients notice appetite changes in the first one to two weeks. Meaningful fat loss typically happens over the first one to three months, with continued progress as your dose is optimised. Results vary with consistency, habits and individual metabolism.

Are labs required? Are they included?+

Quarterly labs are included with your subscription, at any Labcorp, or at home for a small upcharge. We strongly encourage completing them: bloodwork is how your provider knows what to change.

Who might not be a candidate?+

GLP-1 therapy may not be appropriate for people with certain conditions, including a history of medullary thyroid cancer, MEN-2 syndrome, or specific gastrointestinal disorders. Complete your intake and a provider will review your history.

What are the common side effects?+

Most commonly nausea, reduced appetite, bloating, constipation or fatigue, especially during dose increases. Most are mild and temporary, and manageable with proper dosing. Providers can prescribe anti-nausea medication when needed.

Am I locked into a contract? Do I stay on this forever?+

No. Pricing is transparent, billing is month to month, and you can adjust or discontinue with your provider. Many patients move from weight-loss dosing to a microdosing protocol for maintenance and broader health benefits.
